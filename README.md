# Grinding Burn Risk Calculator

A lightweight preview of the **Grinding Burn Risk Calculator** from
[SinoGrind](https://sinogrind.com).

**Live calculator:** https://sinogrind.com/tools/grinding-burn-risk-calculator

## What it does

The full tool screens grinding burn risk from the workpiece material, wheel
type, depth of cut, wheel speed and coolant application. It returns a risk
level, an indicator of specific energy, the likely damage mode and a practical
mitigation checklist.

## Inputs

- Workpiece material (carbon steel, alloy steel, hardened steel, stainless,
  cast iron, aluminium, carbide, ceramic)
- Wheel type and bond
- Depth of cut
- Wheel speed
- Coolant type and delivery
